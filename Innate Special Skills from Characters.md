#  Revenant Job System - Innate Special Augment from Characters
*Character Innate Special Augment Effect*
- Vaan **Thievery:** Enables the theft of superior and rare items.
- Penelo **Veil:** Avoid detection based on magick.
- Basch **Stoneskin:** Reduces damage taken by 30%.
- Fran **Life Cloak:** Avoid detection based on low HP.
- Balthier **Combo Boost:** Improves chance of scoring multiple hits.
- Ashe **Attack Boost:**   Increases Attack damage by 20%.

-RJS Character Feature Chart · Six playable characters · One innate special Augment per character-
(Some Maybe have a-not effect with some equipments, since they are using 100% at the time the Augment)
