# Elemental Magicks — Status Effect Chances

> **Enemy Magicks are not affected.**  
> The percentages below show the chance for each elemental Magick to inflict its additional status effect. Values increase according to the available rank/tier of that Magick.

## Status Effect Chart

| Element | Additional Status | Tier I | Tier II | Tier III | Tier IV |
|:--|:--|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| 🔥 **Fire** | 🔴 **Berserk** | **5%** | **8%** | **12%** | **15%** |
| ❄️ **Ice** | 🧊 **Immobilize** | **6%** | **9%** | **14%** | **20%** |
| ⚡ **Lightning** | 🟡 **Disable** | **4%** | **7%** | **10%** | **14%** |
| 🪨 **Earth** | 🟤 **Slow** | **7%** | **12%** | **30%** | — |
| 🌪️ **Wind** | 🟢 **Silence** | **5%** | **10%** | **14%** | **21%** |
| 💧 **Water** | 🔵 **Oil** | **10%** | **15%** | **22%** | **32%** |
| ✨ **Holy** | 🟡 **Disable** | **12%** | **18%** | **30%** | — |

### Notes

- `—` means that no additional Magick tier is available for that element.
- **Earth** and **Holy** intentionally have three available tiers because of the available in-game Magick slots.
- These percentages apply to the player's elemental Magicks only; enemy Magicks are excluded.
